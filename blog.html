Complete Guide to Whiplash Injuries: Symptoms, Treatment & Recovery

Whiplash injuries from car accidents cause sudden neck trauma affecting muscles, tendons, and nerves. Symptoms include neck pain, headaches, shoulder stiffness, and sometimes cognitive difficulties—often appearing hours or days after impact. Early medical evaluation is crucial for proper diagnosis using X-rays, MRI, or CT scans. Treatment combines rest, physical therapy, chiropractic care, and medications.
