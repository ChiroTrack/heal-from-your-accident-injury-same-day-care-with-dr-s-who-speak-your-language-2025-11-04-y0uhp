Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

Oakland Accident Care ("we," "our," "us," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process your personal information in connection with our website, mobile applications, and services (collectively, the "Services").

We understand that privacy is important to you, and we are dedicated to being transparent about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors and users of our Services. By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this document.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide Directly

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our Services, including:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Medical Information: Health history, symptoms, medical conditions, medications, and injury details
  • Insurance Information: Insurance provider, policy numbers, and coverage details
  • Accident Information: Details about your accident, date, location, and parties involved
  • Payment Information: Billing address and payment method details
  • Communication Preferences: Your preferred method of contact and communication frequency

Information Collected Automatically

When you access our Services, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and device identifiers
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, links clicked, time spent on pages, and referral sources
  • Location Information: General geographic location based on IP address
  • Log Data: Server logs containing access times, pages requested, and error messages

Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources, including:

  • Insurance Companies: Information needed to process insurance claims
  • Healthcare Providers: Medical records and referral information
  • Legal Representatives: Information provided by your attorney or representative
  • Data Providers: Information from publicly available sources and data aggregators

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including:

Medical Care & Treatment

Providing medical evaluation, diagnosis, treatment, and care coordination for your accident injuries. This includes scheduling appointments, managing your medical records, and coordinating with specialists.

Billing & Insurance

Processing insurance claims, managing billing and payment processing, and communicating with insurance providers to ensure proper coverage and reimbursement.

Communication

Sending appointment reminders, treatment updates, follow-up care instructions, and responding to your inquiries and requests.

Service Improvement

Analyzing usage patterns and feedback to improve our Services, develop new features, and enhance the overall quality of care we provide.

Legal Compliance

Complying with legal obligations, maintaining medical records as required by law, and protecting against fraud and legal liability.

Marketing & Updates

Sending promotional materials, wellness tips, and service updates (only if you have opted in to receive such communications).

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us recognize you, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our Services. Cookies can be either session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (remain on your device until they expire or you delete them).

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies

    Required for the basic functioning of our website, such as authentication and security features. These cannot be disabled.

  • Performance Cookies

    Help us understand how visitors use our website by collecting anonymous usage data. This information helps us improve performance and user experience.

  • Functional Cookies

    Remember your preferences and choices (such as language preferences or appointment settings) to provide a personalized experience.

  • Marketing Cookies

    Used to track your interaction with our marketing content and advertisements. You can control these through your cookie preferences.

Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other tracking technologies such as:

  • Web Beacons: Small transparent images embedded in web pages that track page views and user interactions
  • Pixel Tags: Similar to web beacons, used to track email opens and clicks
  • Analytics Tools: Services like Google Analytics that help us understand website usage patterns

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. You can typically choose to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject all cookies except essential ones
  • Be notified when a cookie is being set
  • Delete cookies from your device

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your ability to access certain features.

How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Healthcare Providers & Specialists

    We share medical information with other healthcare providers involved in your treatment to ensure coordinated and comprehensive care.

  • Insurance Companies

    We disclose information necessary to process insurance claims and verify coverage. This is essential for our zero-cost upfront policy.

  • Legal & Law Enforcement

    We may share information when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and the rights of our patients.

  • Service Providers

    We share information with vendors and service providers who assist us in operating our website, processing payments, and managing our business.

  • Business Transfers

    In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

  • With Your Consent

    We may share your information with other third parties only with your explicit consent.

Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement comprehensive physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction.

Our security measures include:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols and access controls
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy practices
  • Compliance with HIPAA and other healthcare regulations

However, no security system is impenetrable. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and account information.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access

    You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.

  • Right to Correction

    You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information we have about you.

  • Right to Deletion

    In certain circumstances, you may request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal and medical record retention requirements.

  • Right to Opt-Out

    You can opt out of receiving marketing communications from us at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in our emails.

  • Right to Data Portability

    You may request a copy of your personal information in a portable format that can be transferred to another service provider.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the Contact Us section below. We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services, fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, and comply with legal obligations. Medical records are retained in accordance with HIPAA requirements and applicable state laws, typically for a minimum of six years following your last visit.

After you request deletion or when the retention period expires, we will securely destroy your information unless we are required to retain it for legal, tax, or legitimate business purposes.

If you wish to request the deletion of your information, please contact us, and we will work with you to determine what information can be deleted while maintaining compliance with applicable regulations.

Third-Party Links

Our Services may contain links to third-party websites and applications that are not operated by Oakland Accident Care. This Privacy Policy does not apply to these third-party services, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites or applications before providing your personal information. Your use of third-party services is subject to their terms and privacy policies, and we recommend that you read them carefully.
