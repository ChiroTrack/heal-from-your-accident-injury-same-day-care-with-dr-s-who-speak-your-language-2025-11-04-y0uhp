About Oakland Accident Care

Oakland Accident Care was founded in 2015 by a team of dedicated healthcare professionals who witnessed firsthand the struggles that accident victims faced when trying to access quality, affordable care. After seeing countless patients navigate fragmented healthcare systems, long wait times, and mounting medical bills, our founders decided to create a comprehensive solution. They established our clinic with a single mission: to provide immediate, coordinated, and compassionate care to everyone injured in accidents, regardless of their background or insurance status. Over the past decade, we've grown from a small practice to a full-service accident recovery center, treating thousands of patients and helping them reclaim their lives.

Our core values are deeply rooted in accessibility, excellence, and empathy. We believe that quality accident care should never be delayed by financial concerns or language barriers. That's why we maintain our zero-cost upfront policy and ensure our team speaks multiple languages. We're committed to treating every patient with the dignity and respect they deserve during one of the most challenging times in their lives. Our multidisciplinary approach—combining medical doctors, chiropractors, physical therapists, and rehabilitation specialists—ensures that each patient receives comprehensive, personalized care. We measure our success not in the number of patients we treat, but in the quality of their recovery and their ability to return to the activities they love.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our accident care services.

First, ensure your safety and the safety of others. If possible, move to a safe location away from traffic. Call emergency services if anyone is injured. Exchange information with other drivers and document the scene with photos. Then, contact Oakland Accident Care as soon as possible—we offer same-day appointments to begin your assessment and treatment. Early medical evaluation is crucial to identify all injuries, even those that may not be immediately apparent.

Don't wait to feel pain or symptoms. Some injuries like whiplash or internal injuries may take hours or days to manifest. Our experienced doctors will conduct a thorough examination and create a treatment plan to prevent long-term complications.

We understand that accident injuries come with enough stress without worrying about immediate medical bills. Our zero cost upfront policy means you receive all necessary medical care, diagnostics, treatment, and therapy without paying anything out of pocket at the time of service.

Here's how it works: We work directly with your insurance company to handle all billing and claims. If you're involved in a car accident, your auto insurance typically covers medical treatment through your Personal Injury Protection (PIP) coverage or the at-fault party's liability insurance. We manage all the paperwork and communication with insurance providers, so you can focus entirely on your recovery.

If you don't have insurance or your coverage is limited, we have payment plans and other options available. We never turn away patients due to financial concerns.

Oakland Accident Care specializes in treating all types of injuries resulting from car accidents, including:

  • Whiplash and neck injuries: Common in rear-end collisions
  • Back and spinal injuries: From impact trauma
  • Soft tissue injuries: Sprains, strains, and contusions
  • Fractures and broken bones: With advanced imaging and orthopedic care
  • Concussions and head injuries: Neurological assessment and treatment
  • Internal injuries: Comprehensive diagnostic evaluation

Our coordinated care team develops personalized treatment plans that may include medical treatment, physical therapy, chiropractic care, and rehabilitation services.

Recovery time varies significantly depending on the severity and type of injuries sustained. Some patients recover in a few weeks, while others may require several months of comprehensive treatment. Here's what we typically observe:

  • Minor injuries: 2-4 weeks with consistent treatment
  • Moderate injuries: 4-12 weeks of coordinated care
  • Severe injuries: 3-12 months or longer

The key to faster recovery is early intervention and consistent treatment. Starting care immediately after an accident and following your treatment plan diligently significantly improves outcomes.
